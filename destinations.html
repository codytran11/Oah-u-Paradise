Hilton Waikiki

This is the hotel that I stayed at. I believe this is a great budget option as the hotel was only around $400 for 4 nights (though I did sleep with 5 people for 2 beds). As you can tell by the pictures, the view is pretty breathtaking and the room was spacious enough for us — I'd highly recommend the Hilton Waikiki.

Waimea Waterfalls

Waimea Waterfalls is an amazing tourist destination. With its beautiful hike spots and the amazing waterfall, it is a must-see place. You can pay $20 to swim in the waterfall. Definitely recommended!

Kailua Beach

Kailua Beach was the best beach that I visited during my Hawaii trip. I visited during the sunset and it was the most beautiful view of the trip. We swam in the ocean too and it was the perfect temperature. Also it wasn't that crowded. If I were to choose any spot that you had to visit, it would be Kailua Beach.

Ala Moana Mall

Every vacation deserves a shopping spree. Ala Moana Mall is the shopping mall of Hawaii itself. This place has every store you could ever ask for. The views are amazing and since it is outdoors, the atmosphere is even better.
